## Key Findings

- Study hours showed the strongest positive relationship with exam score.
- Mental health rating had a moderate positive relationship with academic performance.
- Social media and Netflix usage showed weak negative correlations with exam scores.
- The Linear Regression model achieved approximately **90% R²**, indicating strong predictive performance.
- The Decision Tree classifier achieved approximately **88% accuracy** for Pass/Fail prediction.
